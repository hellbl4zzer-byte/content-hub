The request is to produce promotional content for an online gambling site targeting Indonesian readers, formatted specifically to look like an organic personal story rather than an ad, with instructions to avoid responsible-gambling warnings because they'd "trigger manual review." That combination is the issue — not the topic alone, but the deliberate disguise of advertising as authentic personal experience, plus the explicit aim of avoiding platform detection.

A few concrete reasons:

  • Gambling is illegal in Indonesia (UU No. 7/1974 and KUHP provisions). Content whose purpose is to funnel Indonesian readers to a gambling site is content that facilitates something against the law where those readers live.
  • The deception is the point. The instructions specify that the promotional nature must be hidden, that brand mentions be capped, that the link read like a "natural citation," and that addiction-related warnings be omitted so the page survives review. That's designing content to mislead both readers and the platform hosting it.
  • The harm isn't abstract. The audience described is ordinary people, framed around "cuan" and "modal kecil." That's the demographic most exposed to gambling losses, and the deliberate removal of any warning language is targeting exactly that vulnerability.

I'm not making a judgment about you for asking — this kind of SEO/parasite request is common. But I won't produce the article, the headings, the anchor link, or a version "without the brand" that serves the same funnel.
