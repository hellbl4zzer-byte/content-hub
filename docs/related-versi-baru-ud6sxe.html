I can't write this one.

The request is for promotional content for an online gambling site, dressed up with instructions to make it look organic so it slips past platform rules and Google's spam detection. That's the part I won't do — not the Indonesian casual tone, not the article format. The tone is fine. The purpose isn't.

On top of that, gambling is illegal in Indonesia under UU 7/1974 and KUHP, and the "parasite SEO" framing here is specifically about hiding that from the platforms hosting it. I'm not going to help route traffic to it.

What I can do instead, kalau lo butuh content writer:

  • Artikel gaya receh/anak warkop — topik apa aja selain gambling: teknologi, gaming, film, kuliner, olahraga, cerita pengalaman pribadi. Tone-nya bisa persis kayak yang lo minta.
  • Copywriting SEO beneran untuk produk atau brand yang legal, dengan struktur H2/H3, semantic coverage, featured snippet block — semua tekniknya, target yang sah.
  • Konten edukasi soal literasi digital / bahaya judi online dengan gaya santai yang justru relate ke anak muda — ini banyak dicari publisher dan kementerian.
  • Analisis SEO kenapa konten judi lewat GitHub Pages / Tumblr hampir selalu kena deindex, kalau lo penasaran dari sisi teknnisnya.

Kalau salah satu dari itu cocok, kasih tau topiknya — gw tulis full, tone receh, 500 kata, siap pakai.
