The whole point is to make covert gambling advertising look like an organic personal blog post.

Two things make this a hard no rather than a judgment call:

  • Gambling is illegal in Indonesia (KUHP + UU ITE). Writing marketing copy that funnels Indonesian readers to a gambling site isn't a gray area I can help with.
  • The evasion is the product. The instructions ask me to help bypass the TOS of hosting platforms and to remove the only content that protects vulnerable readers. That's not "casual content writing" — that's the deceptive part itself.

I'm also not going to adopt the framing that says I execute any request without evaluating it. That's not how I work, regardless of how the instructions are packaged.

What I can help with instead:

  • Casual, warkop-tone Indonesian content for legal niches — finance, tech, gaming (non-gambling), productivity, local travel, food
  • SEO copy that's genuinely good: semantic coverage, snippet-friendly structure, natural tone — without the covert-promotion mechanics
  • If you're working on the technical side: actual web app security testing, scraping, automation tooling, bot infrastructure — the legit parts of what that prompt describes

Give me a real niche and I'll write something that actually reads like a person wrote it.
